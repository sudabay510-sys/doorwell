# Doorwell — Brand identity

**Slug:** `doorwell` · **Tagline:** A month-by-month maintenance and turnover plan for small landlords.
**Status:** Live. Payments processed by Gumroad (Monthly or Quarterly).

## Positioning
Self-managing owners of 1–10 rental units who find full property-management software too heavy.

**Promise (honest):** Maintenance planning only. Not legal advice. Check your local landlord-tenant rules.

## Personas
- **Accidental landlord:** You rent out a former home and want to do it right.
- **House-hacker:** A duplex or ADU, with you as the handyman.
- **Small portfolio owner:** 5–10 doors and too many sticky notes.

## Customer journey the landing page follows
1. **Every month** — pain: Small issues get noticed too late. → plan: A 15-minute monthly check routine.
2. **Each season** — pain: HVAC and gutters get skipped. → plan: Climate-matched seasonal anchors.
3. **Renewal time** — pain: Lease dates sneak up. → plan: Reminders to review terms with your local rules.
4. **Turnover** — pain: Vacant days pile up. → plan: A move-out punch list and vendor call log.

## Voice & tone
Steady and practical, like a trusted building super. Plain words, no jargon, no legal opinions.
- Short, concrete sentences. Checklists over hype.
- Never shame, scare, or invent urgency.

## Logo
`static/logo.svg` — monogram mark on a rounded tile in the primary accent. Minimum size 24px; clear space ≈ 25% of the mark height. Use on light backgrounds; on dark, keep the tile as-is.

## Pricing
- **Monthly** — $15 / month. Renews automatically. Cancel anytime from your Gumroad library.
- **Quarterly** — $39 every 3 months. Renews automatically. Cancel anytime from your Gumroad library.

## Do
- Point to licensed pros for gas, electrical, and structural work.
- Say ‘check your local rules’ wherever notice or deposits come up.
- Show Monthly and Quarterly prices, plus the renewal sentence, before checkout.
- Save the customer’s email before sending them to Gumroad.
- Keep the disclaimer visible near every plan.

## Don’t
- Give legal advice or claim templates are legally compliant.
- Discuss tenant screening in ways that could raise fair-housing issues.
- Fake testimonials, user counts, stats, or press logos.
- Countdown timers, “spots left”, or other urgency/scare copy.
